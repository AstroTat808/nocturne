Privacy
Notice.

Effective date: August 12, 2026. This notice describes how Wild Ones LLC and NOCTURNE Festival use information submitted through this site.

Information we collect

Application information may include your name, preferred name, email address, phone number, location, social profile or handle, referral source, written application responses, group information you choose to provide, and your consent selections.

How we use it

We use application information to review requests for event access, verify applicants, administer invitations, communicate event information, protect event and community safety, prevent abuse or fraud, and maintain records connected to admission decisions. Marketing communications are optional and tied to the separate opt-in on the form.

Selection criteria and protected information

The application is intended to assess event fit, community standards, verification, safety, and capacity. Applicants should not submit sensitive information about race, religion, disability, medical history, sexual orientation, gender identity, or other protected characteristics unless it is voluntarily relevant to an accommodation request. Selection decisions should not be based on protected characteristics.

Service providers

We may use hosting, form-processing, communications, ticketing, security, analytics, and event operations providers to process information on our behalf for the purposes described above.

Retention and security

We keep application data only as long as reasonably necessary for review, event administration, safety, recordkeeping, and legal obligations. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information and the service.

Your choices

You may opt out of promotional communications at any time through the method provided in those messages. Questions or requests regarding application information can be directed through the official NOCTURNE/Wild Ones contact channel designated for the event.

Changes

We may update this notice as the event systems and practices evolve. The current version will be posted on this page with its effective date.
