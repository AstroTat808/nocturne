Access Rules

Site & Invitation
Terms.

Effective date: August 12, 2026. These terms govern use of the NOCTURNE Festival website and invitation process operated by Wild Ones LLC.

Applications are requests, not reservations

Submitting an application does not create a right to attend, hold inventory, reserve a ticket, or guarantee approval. Applications may be accepted or declined based on lawful event criteria including capacity, verification, community standards, safety, and operational requirements.

Invitation access

Invitation codes and private ticket links are confidential, may be limited or single-use, and are intended only for the approved recipient. Sharing, reselling, duplicating, circumventing, or attempting to automate access may result in cancellation or denied admission.

Tickets and event terms

Any ticket purchase is also subject to the ticketing provider’s terms, refund rules, venue requirements, event-specific rules, age restrictions if applicable, and any terms presented at checkout or before entry.

Drink packages

A drink package is available only to guests who are 21 or older and present valid photo identification. It is tied to the named ticket holder, activated to a staff-issued wristband, and may not be transferred or shared. Each credit covers one eligible beer, well cocktail, or non-alcoholic substitute; premium cocktails require the stated upgrade payment. Unused credits expire when event bar service ends and have no cash value. Possession of credits never guarantees alcohol service: bartenders may delay or refuse service to comply with law, licensing conditions, safety requirements, and responsible-service judgment.

Changes and cancellations

Event details, programming, lineup, location, schedule, access conditions, and availability may change. Any rights regarding refunds or credits will be governed by the terms associated with the applicable ticket purchase and event announcement.

Conduct

Guests must comply with the NOCTURNE Code of Conduct, venue rules, staff instructions, and applicable law. Access may be denied or revoked for violations.

Site use

Do not probe, scrape, interfere with, reverse engineer, overload, bypass, or attempt unauthorized access to the site, invitation system, forms, or ticket pathways.
