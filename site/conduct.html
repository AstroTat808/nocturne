Community Standard

Code of
Conduct.

NOCTURNE is built around respect, consent, personal expression, and the shared responsibility to protect the experience for everyone present.

Respect and consent

Respect personal boundaries. Consent must be clear, voluntary, informed, and ongoing. Harassment, intimidation, unwanted touching, stalking, coercion, or predatory conduct is not permitted.

No discrimination or hate

Targeted abuse, threats, discriminatory conduct, or hateful behavior toward another guest, artist, worker, vendor, or community member is grounds for removal and loss of future access.

Protect the room

Do not interfere with performers, production, staff, or safety operations. Follow venue and event instructions. Do not bring prohibited items or engage in conduct that creates an unreasonable risk to others.

Privacy

Respect the privacy of other guests. Do not use another person’s image or private information in a way that violates their consent or safety. Event-specific photo/video rules may be communicated before entry.

Access may be revoked

Wild Ones/NOCTURNE may deny or revoke access for conduct inconsistent with these standards, event rules, venue requirements, or applicable law.
